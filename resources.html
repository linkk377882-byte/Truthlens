01.

Verification Hub

This module provides practical tools for fast media authenticity checks.

AI Automatic Detection: Use tools such as Deepware, Reality Defender, and Hive to scan synthetic traces in video, audio, and images.

Traceability Search: Use Google Lens and TinEye to track original upload contexts and distribution history.

Manual Verification: Cross-check findings against Snopes, Reuters, and other professional fact-checking organizations.

02.

AI & Artifacts

This module explains how synthetic media is produced and what kinds of artifacts it tends to leave behind.

Understanding the Source: Learn how GPT, Stable Diffusion, and newer video models generate text, imagery, and clips.

Identify Defects: Train your eye to catch asymmetry, texture failure, temporal glitches, and other digital fingerprints.

03.

Digital Literacy

This module focuses on long-term verification habits and modern content-integrity standards.

SIFT Method: Stop, investigate the source, find better coverage, and trace claims back to the original context.

Industry Standards: Learn how C2PA content credentials can reveal provenance, editing history, and AI involvement.
